Data

Everything is stored in this browser only. Export a backup regularly, and use it to move your data to another device or share it with someone helping you train.

IRONLOG runs entirely client-side. Calorie/BMR figures use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation; calorie-burn estimates use standard MET coefficients. These are population-average estimates, not individualized medical advice — treat targets as a starting point and adjust based on your real-world results over 2–3 weeks.
